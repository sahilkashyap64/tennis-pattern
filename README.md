# tennis-pattern

## What's in the game?

### Start Here — Beginner Roadmap

Seven interactive lessons introduce tennis rules, court markings, scoring,
strokes, shot selection, and basic strategy. Learn serving and receiving,
games and sets, basic grips, footwork, recovery, and court etiquette. Each
lesson includes optional deeper explanations, a simple practice exercise,
and a quick quiz. The roadmap ends with a visual decision flowchart for
choosing a response to the ball.

### Tennis Dictionary

Learn 37 essential tennis terms in simple English, with examples and memory
tricks to make the vocabulary stick.

### Memory Dojo

Build recall with eight beginner concepts and 36 tactical flashcards. Visual
mnemonics, chess-style pattern recognition, active recall, and repeated
practice turn tactical ideas into habits.

### Pattern Arena

Play the interactive court game and attach a memorable mental picture to each
tactical lesson.

## Training and replay features

- 36 lessons and 55 tactical decisions.
- Choose both the shot and target, including serves, slices, volleys, lobs,
  and drop shots.
- Progress through Learn, Groove, and Match IQ training modes.
- Get playing-style recommendations for four player types.
- Follow court animations, tactical explanations, XP, and saved progress.
- See references to the book's pattern numbers, pages, and related on-court
  drills.

Animated demonstrations make each decision easier to picture:

- A side view shows the ball's trajectory over the net and where it lands.
- Lobs travel in high arcs, slices stay lower, and drop shots land short.
- Pause, resume, replay, or adjust playback speed from 0.65× to 2×.
- Jump from the beginner roadmap directly into an animated demonstration.
- Replay the shot and target you selected after answering.
