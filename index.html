THE MEMORY DOJO

Train your eyes like a chess player.

Experts recognize familiar shapes and situations. Tennis works the same way: spot the cue → remember the plan → choose the shot. Guess before flipping each card; repeat the ones you missed.

3 ways to make tactics stick

👀
1 · See the cue

Look for the opponent’s position, your balance and the bounce.

🎬
2 · Make a silly movie

A serve opens a door; a crosscourt shot is a seatbelt. Strange pictures are easier to recall.

♟️
3 · Chunk the sequence

Remember one motif—pull → pounce—instead of memorizing ten disconnected movements.
